# Preface

## Purpose

This Book defines the Core Platform of the Universal Service ERP.

The Core Platform contains reusable services shared by all business modules. It is responsible for providing common capabilities while remaining independent of any specific business domain.

Business logic belongs to business modules, not to the Core Platform.

---

# Summary

The Core Platform establishes a consistent and reusable foundation for the entire system.
