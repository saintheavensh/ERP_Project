# Role Management

## Purpose

This document defines how user roles are managed.

---

# Responsibilities

The Role Management service is responsible for:

- Create role
- Update role
- Delete role
- Assign permissions
- Assign users
- Manage role hierarchy (future support)

---

# Examples

Typical roles include:

- Super Administrator
- Business Owner
- Branch Manager
- Administrator
- Customer Service
- Technician
- Warehouse Staff
- Purchasing Staff
- Cashier
- Accountant

Businesses may create additional custom roles.

---

# Rules

- A role represents a collection of permissions.
- Users may be assigned one or more roles.
- Roles must remain independent of business modules.

---

# Summary

Role Management simplifies access control by grouping permissions into reusable roles.
