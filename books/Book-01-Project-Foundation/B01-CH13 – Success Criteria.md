# Success Criteria

## Purpose

This document defines how the success of the Universal Service ERP project will be evaluated.

---

# Business Success

The project is considered successful when:

- Businesses can manage daily operations through one integrated platform.
- Business processes become more efficient.
- Operational data is centralized.
- Reports support better decision making.

---

# Technical Success

The project is considered technically successful when:

- The platform remains modular.
- New modules can be added without redesigning the core platform.
- Documentation and implementation remain synchronized.
- The system remains maintainable and scalable.

---

# Long-Term Success

Long-term success is achieved when:

- Multiple service industries use the same platform.
- New business domains are supported through additional modules.
- The platform continues evolving without major architectural changes.

---

# Summary

These success criteria provide measurable goals for evaluating the project's progress and long-term sustainability.
