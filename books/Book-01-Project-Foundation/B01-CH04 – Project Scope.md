# Project Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the scope of the Universal Service ERP project.

---

# Scope

The project includes the design and specification of a modular ERP platform for service-oriented businesses.

The platform covers:

- Customer Management
- Device Management
- Service Management
- Technician Management
- Inventory Management
- Purchasing Management
- Sales Management
- Supplier Management
- Financial Management
- Reporting
- User and Role Management
- Dashboard
- Notifications
- System Configuration
- Audit Logs

The platform is designed to support multiple service industries through configurable modules.

---

# Supported Business Model

The first implementation targets mobile phone service centers.

The platform architecture must allow future support for:

- Laptop service
- Printer service
- Electronics repair
- Appliance repair
- Automotive workshops
- Other service industries

without redesigning the core platform.

---

# Summary

The project scope defines the functional boundaries of the Universal Service ERP platform while keeping the architecture extensible for future industries.
