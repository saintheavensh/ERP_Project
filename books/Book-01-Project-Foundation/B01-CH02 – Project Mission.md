# Project Mission

## Purpose

This document defines the mission of the Universal Service ERP project.

---

# Mission

The mission of Universal Service ERP is to simplify business operations by providing an integrated, configurable, and maintainable management platform that supports daily operational activities across different service industries.

---

# Mission Objectives

The platform should:

- Reduce manual work.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Centralize business data.
- Improve reporting accuracy.
- Support business growth.
- Reduce implementation complexity through reusable modules.

---

# Summary

The mission focuses on helping service businesses operate more efficiently through a single integrated platform.
