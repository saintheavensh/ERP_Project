# Target Users

## Purpose

This document defines the primary users of the Universal Service ERP platform.

---

# Primary Users

The platform is intended for:

- Business Owner
- Branch Manager
- Administrator
- Customer Service
- Technician
- Warehouse Staff
- Purchasing Staff
- Sales Staff
- Cashier
- Accountant

---

# Secondary Users

Additional users may include:

- Customers
- Suppliers
- Auditors
- External Partners

These users may have limited access depending on business requirements.

---

# User Characteristics

Target users may have different levels of technical knowledge.

The platform should therefore provide:

- Simple navigation
- Consistent interface
- Clear terminology
- Efficient workflows

---

# Summary

The platform is designed to support users across different operational roles while maintaining a consistent user experience.
