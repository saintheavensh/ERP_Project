# Business Domains

## Purpose

This document defines the business domains supported by the Universal Service ERP platform.

---

# Core Domains

The platform consists of the following business domains:

- Customer Management
- Device Management
- Service Management
- Technician Management
- Inventory Management
- Purchasing Management
- Sales Management
- Supplier Management
- Financial Management
- Reporting
- Dashboard
- User Management
- Role & Permission Management
- Notification Management
- System Configuration
- Audit Management

Each domain is designed as an independent module while remaining integrated with the core platform.

---

# Future Domains

The platform architecture allows future domains such as:

- Warranty Management
- CRM
- Asset Management
- Rental Management
- Membership
- Multi Branch
- Multi Company
- Business Intelligence
- Third-party Integration

These domains are outside the initial implementation but are supported by the platform architecture.

---

# Summary

Business domains represent the major functional areas of the Universal Service ERP platform.
