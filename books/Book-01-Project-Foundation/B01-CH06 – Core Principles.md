# Core Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the core principles that guide the design and evolution of the Universal Service ERP platform.

---

# Principles

## Modular Architecture

The platform is built as independent modules connected through a shared core.

---

## Configuration Over Customization

Business differences should be handled through configuration whenever possible instead of modifying the core system.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Business information must have one authoritative source.

---

## Documentation First

Business requirements must be documented before implementation begins.

---

## Scalability

The platform should support future growth without requiring major architectural changes.

---

## Maintainability

The system should remain easy to understand, modify, and extend.

---

## Security by Design

Security considerations should be incorporated into every module from the beginning rather than added later.

---

## Consistency

Business rules, workflows, terminology, and user experience should remain consistent across all modules.

---

## Reusability

Shared functionality should be implemented once and reused throughout the platform.

---

## Extensibility

Future modules should integrate with the platform without modifying existing modules whenever possible.

---

# Summary

These principles serve as the foundation for all business and technical decisions throughout the lifecycle of the Universal Service ERP platform.
