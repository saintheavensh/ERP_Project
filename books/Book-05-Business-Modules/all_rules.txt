[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] # Lifecycle Rules
[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] ## Rule 1
Every lifecycle must define a starting state.
---
[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] ## Rule 2
Every lifecycle must define one or more terminal states.
Examples:
* Closed
* Cancelled
---
[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] ## Rule 3
State transitions must be controlled.
Users must not bypass required lifecycle stages.
---
[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] ## Rule 4
All transitions must be recorded in the audit trail.
---
[B05-CORE-003 Business Lifecycle Framework.md] ## Rule 5
Business rules determine whether a transition is allowed.
---
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] # Status Governance Rules
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] ## Rule 1
Every business entity must define an initial status.
---
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] ## Rule 2
Every business entity must define at least one terminal status.
Examples:
* Closed
* Cancelled
---
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] ## Rule 3
Status transitions must follow approved workflows.
Direct status jumping should not be allowed unless explicitly authorized.
---
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] ## Rule 4
Every status transition must be recorded.
The following information should be captured:
* Previous Status
* New Status
* Date and Time
* User or Process
* Reason (if applicable)
---
[B05-CORE-004 Status Management Framework.md] ## Rule 5
Historical status records must be immutable.
---

[B05-CORE-009 Business Rules Framework.md] # Rule Hierarchy
Business rules may inherit from higher-level policies.
```text id="br12"

        ↓
Business Rule
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Workflow Action
```

```text id="br13"

        ↓
FIFO Rule
        ↓
Stock Validation
```

[B05-CORE-009 Business Rules Framework.md] # Rule Lifecycle
Business rules follow a controlled lifecycle.
```text id="br14"
Draft
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approved
    ↓
Active
    ↓
Retired
```
Rules should not be implemented without approval.

[B05-SVC-011 Service Business Rules.md] # Audit Rules
The following records must never be deleted:
* Intake Records
* Service Orders
* Estimates
* Approvals
* QC Records
* Completion Records
* Cancellation Records
---
[B05-SVC-011 Service Business Rules.md] # Historical Integrity Rule
The platform should prioritize:
```text
Historical Preservation
```
over physical deletion.
Soft deletion and status changes are preferred.
