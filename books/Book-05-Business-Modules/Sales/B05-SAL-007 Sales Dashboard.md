# B05-SAL-007

# Sales Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines retail sales visibility, sales monitoring, and retail performance analytics.

---

# Business Philosophy

Management should understand sales performance quickly and accurately.

---

# Dashboard Components

---

## Sales Summary

Displays:

* Daily Sales
* Weekly Sales
* Monthly Sales

---

## Revenue Summary

Displays:

* Gross Revenue
* Net Revenue
* Refund Amount

---

## Product Analysis

Displays:

* Top Selling Products
* Slow Moving Products

---

## Category Analysis

Displays:

* Spare Parts
* Accessories
* Devices

---

## Customer Analysis

Displays:

* New Customers
* Returning Customers

---

## Payment Analysis

Displays:

* Cash
* Transfer
* QRIS
* Other Methods

---

## Return Analysis

Displays:

* Return Count
* Return Value

---

## Discount Analysis

Displays:

* Discount Amount
* Discount Frequency

---

# Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Manager
* Sales Supervisor

---

# Summary

Sales Dashboard provides operational and financial visibility into retail sales performance.
