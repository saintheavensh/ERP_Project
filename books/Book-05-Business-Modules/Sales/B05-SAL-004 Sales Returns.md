# Sales Return Management

## Purpose

Defines retail return processes, refund handling, exchange transactions, and customer credits.

---

# Business Philosophy

Returns should be controlled, traceable, and financially accurate.

---

# Return Reasons

Supports:

### Defective Product

---

### Wrong Product

---

### Customer Exchange

---

### Other Approved Reasons

---

# Return Settlement Methods

Supports:

### Refund

Customer receives money back.

---

### Exchange

Returned product replaced.

---

### Store Credit

Value converted to customer credit balance.

---

# Return Workflow

```text id="srm001"
Return Request
↓
Validation
↓
Approval
↓
Settlement
```

# Inventory Integration

Returned products may:

* Return To Stock
* Become Damaged Stock
* Become Quarantine Stock

---

# Finance Integration

Supports:

* Refund Entries
* Revenue Adjustments
* Store Credit Liability

# Audit Trail

* Settlement Method

Sales Return Management controls retail return processing and settlement.
