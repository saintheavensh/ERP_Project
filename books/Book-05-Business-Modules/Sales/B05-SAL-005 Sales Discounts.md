# B05-SAL-005

# Sales Discount Management

## Purpose

Defines retail discount policies and discount controls.

---

# Business Philosophy

Discounts should increase sales while remaining controlled and auditable.

---

# Supported Discount Types

### Percentage Discount

Example:

```text id="sd001"
10%
```

---

### Fixed Amount Discount

Example:

```text id="sd002"
Rp 50.000
```

---

### Item Discount

Applied to specific products.

---

### Transaction Discount

Applied to entire transaction.

---

# Approval Rules

Configurable.

Supports:

* No Approval
* Approval Required Above Limit

---

# Discount Visibility

Displays:

* Original Price
* Discount
* Final Price

---

# Reporting

Provides:

* Discount Usage
* Discount Value
* Discount Frequency

---

# Audit Trail

Track:

* Cashier
* Approver
* Discount Amount

---

# Summary

Sales Discount Management controls retail discount activities.
