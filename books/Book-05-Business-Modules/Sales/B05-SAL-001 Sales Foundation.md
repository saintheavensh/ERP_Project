# B05-SAL-001

# Sales Foundation

## Purpose

Defines retail sales architecture, workflows, and module integrations.

---

# Business Philosophy

Retail sales should be fast, accurate, and inventory-driven.

---

# Scope

This module handles:

* Spare Parts Sales
* Accessories Sales
* Device Sales
* Other Retail Products

---

# Exclusions

Not handled here:

* Service Billing
* Warranty Claims
* Service Payments

---

# Module Relationships

Sales integrates with:

* Inventory
* Customer
* Finance
* Cash Management

---

# Sales Workflow

```text id="sf001"
Product Selection
↓
Checkout
↓
Payment
↓
Inventory Reduction
↓
Revenue Recording
```
