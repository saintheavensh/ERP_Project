# B05-SAL-008

# Sales Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official retail sales rules.

---

# Rule 1

Every completed sale must generate a sales record.

---

# Rule 2

Completed sales update inventory.

---

# Rule 3

Sales and Service Billing are separate modules.

---

# Rule 4

Sales payments support multiple payment methods.

---

# Rule 5

Mixed payments are supported.

---

# Rule 6

Discount policies are configurable.

---

# Rule 7

Sales returns require audit tracking.

---

# Rule 8

Returns support:

* Refund
* Exchange
* Store Credit

---

# Rule 9

Store Credit remains traceable.

---

# Rule 10

Customer registration is optional.

---

# Rule 11

Sales reports remain auditable.

---

# Rule 12

Inventory validation occurs before sale completion.

---

# Rule 13

Reserved inventory cannot be sold.

---

# Rule 14

All financial impacts must be recorded.

---

# Rule 15

All sales activities require audit logging.

---

# Summary

Sales Business Rules ensure consistency, inventory accuracy, and financial control.
