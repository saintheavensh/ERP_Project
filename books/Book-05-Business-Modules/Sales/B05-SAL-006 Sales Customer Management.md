# B05-SAL-006

# Sales Customer Management

## Purpose

Defines customer relationships within retail sales transactions.

---

# Business Philosophy

Customer information should improve service and business visibility.

---

# Customer Types

### Walk-In Customer

No registration required.

---

### Registered Customer

Stored in Customer Module.

---

# Customer Benefits

Supports:

* Purchase History
* Store Credit
* Customer Analysis

---

# Sales Integration

Displays:

* Previous Purchases
* Return History
* Store Credit Balance

---

# Customer Metrics

Supports:

* Total Purchases
* Purchase Frequency
* Lifetime Value

---

# Future Expansion

Supports future:

* Membership Programs
* Loyalty Programs
* Reward Systems

---

# Summary

Sales Customer Management connects retail sales with customer relationship tracking.
