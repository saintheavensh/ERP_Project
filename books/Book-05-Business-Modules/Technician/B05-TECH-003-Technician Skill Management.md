# B05-TECH-003

# Technician Skill Management

## Purpose

Defines technician capabilities, skill assignment, and job matching.

---

# Business Philosophy

Not every technician can repair every device.

Assignments should consider technician capabilities.

---

# Skill Assignment

Supports assigning:

* Repair Categories
* Device Categories
* Brand Specializations

---

# Example Skills

### Apple Specialist

Supports:

* iPhone
* iPad

---

### Android Specialist

Supports:

* Samsung
* Xiaomi
* Oppo
* Vivo

---

### Software Specialist

Supports:

* Flashing
* Unlocking
* Data Recovery

---

### Hardware Specialist

Supports:

* Board Repair
* IC Replacement
* Micro Soldering
