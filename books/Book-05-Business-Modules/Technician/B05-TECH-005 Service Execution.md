# B05-TECH-005

# Service Execution

## Purpose

Defines technician activities during repair execution.

---

# Business Philosophy

Repair progress should be visible without creating excessive administrative work.

---

# Service Execution Workflow

```text id="exec001"
Assigned
↓
Diagnosis
↓
Repair
↓
Testing
↓
Completed
```

---

# Diagnosis Phase

Technician may record:

* Findings
* Root Cause
* Required Parts
* Repair Estimate

---

# Repair Phase

Technician performs:

* Component Replacement
* Software Repair
* Board Repair
* Other Service Activities

---

# Spare Part Usage

Technician may:

* Request Parts
* Use Reserved Parts
* Return Unused Parts

---

# Repair Notes

Supports:

* Internal Notes
* Technical Notes

---

# Customer Notes

Supports customer-visible notes.

---
