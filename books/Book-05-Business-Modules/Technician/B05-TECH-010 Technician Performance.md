# B05-TECH-010

# Technician Performance Management

## Purpose

Defines technician performance measurement, productivity tracking, and operational reporting.

---

# Business Philosophy

Performance measurement should help improve operations, not punish technicians.

---

# Core Principle

Performance data is a management tool.

It should be used to improve:

* Productivity
* Repair Quality
* Customer Satisfaction

---

# Performance Indicators

Supports:

### Completed Repairs

Number of completed jobs.

---

### Average Repair Time

Average time from assignment to completion.

---

### Waiting Parts Impact

Tracks jobs delayed by parts shortages.

---

### Quality Control Pass Rate

Measures repair quality.

---

### Warranty Return Rate

Measures repairs returning during warranty periods.

---

### Rework Rate

Tracks repeat repairs.

---

### Customer Satisfaction

Future enhancement.

---

# Technician Ranking

Supported.

System may rank technicians using configurable scoring.

---

# Performance Periods

Supports:

* Daily
* Weekly
* Monthly
* Quarterly
* Yearly

---

# Management Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Manager
* Service Supervisor

---

# Dashboard Integration

Provides:

* Top Technicians
* Repair Volume
* Quality Metrics

---

# Audit Rules

Performance calculations must remain traceable.

---

# Summary

Performance Management provides measurable operational insights while supporting technician development.
