# B05-TECH-009

# Quality Control Management

## Purpose

Defines testing and verification processes before customer delivery.

---

# Business Philosophy

Repair completion does not guarantee repair quality.

Every completed repair should pass quality control.

---

# Workflow

```text id="qc001"
Repair Completed
↓
Quality Control
↓
Pass
↓
Ready For Pickup
```

---

# Failed QC

If QC fails:

```text id="qc002"
Quality Control
↓
Failed
↓
Back To Technician
```
