# B05-TECH-011

# Technician Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines dashboard views and operational workspaces for technicians.

---

# Business Philosophy

Technicians should immediately see what requires action.

---

# Dashboard Layout

---

## My Assigned Jobs

Displays:

* Assigned Service Orders
* Priority
* Due Dates

---

## Waiting Parts Queue

Displays:

* Waiting Parts Jobs
* Outstanding Requests
* Part Status

---

## Quality Control Queue

Displays:

* Pending QC
* Failed QC
* Rework Requests

---

## Today's Activities

Displays:

* Completed Jobs
* Active Jobs
* Pending Jobs

---

## Notifications

Displays:

* New Assignments
* Part Arrivals
* Reimbursement Updates
* Service Updates

---

# Quick Actions

Supports:

* Start Repair
* Update Status
* Request Parts
* Submit External Purchase
* Submit Reimbursement
* Complete Repair

---

# Mobile Friendly

Required.

Technicians frequently work away from desks.

---

# Personal Metrics

Displays:

* Completed Repairs
* Average Repair Time
* QC Pass Rate

---

# Dashboard Visibility

Technicians see only their own operational information unless granted additional permissions.

---

# Summary

Technician Dashboard serves as the primary workspace for repair operations.
