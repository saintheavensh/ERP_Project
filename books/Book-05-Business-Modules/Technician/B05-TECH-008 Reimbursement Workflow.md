# B05-TECH-008

# Technician Reimbursement Workflow

## Purpose

Defines reimbursement handling when technicians use personal funds for business purchases.

---

# Business Philosophy

Technicians should not suffer financial loss while helping complete customer repairs.

---

# Trigger Condition

Activated when:

```text id="rb001"
Technician Uses Personal Money
```

for business purposes.

---

# Workflow

```text id="rb002"
Technician Purchase
↓
Receipt Submission
↓
Verification
↓
Approval
↓
Reimbursement
```

---

# Required Documents

Supports:

* Receipt Image
* Purchase Details
* Service Order Reference

---

# Approval Process

Approvers may include:

* Owner
* Manager
* Finance Staff

---

# Payment Methods

Supports:

* Cash
* Bank Transfer
* E-Wallet

---

# Reimbursement Status

Supported:

```text id="rb003"
Draft
```

---

```text id="rb004"
Pending Approval
```

---

```text id="rb005"
Approved
```

---

```text id="rb006"
Rejected
```

---

```text id="rb007"
Paid
```

---

# Finance Integration

Automatically creates reimbursement records.

---

# Audit Trail

Track:

* Technician
* Approver
* Amount
* Payment Date

---

# Summary

Reimbursement Workflow protects technicians while maintaining financial accountability.
