# Operational Dashboard

## Purpose

Provides operational teams with day-to-day workload visibility.

---

# Business Philosophy

Operational staff should focus on actions, not reports.

---

* Waiting Diagnosis
* Waiting Approval
* Waiting Re-Approval
* Waiting Parts
* In Progress
* QC Queue

---

# Inventory Section

* Low Stock
* Reserved Inventory
* Incoming Stock

---

# Purchasing Section

Displays:

* Pending Requests
* Open Purchase Orders
* Backorders

---

# Technician Section

Displays:

* Assigned Jobs
* Completed Jobs
* Warranty Jobs

---

# Customer Section

* Ready For Pickup
* Unclaimed Devices

Future configurable policy.

Operations Team
Technicians
