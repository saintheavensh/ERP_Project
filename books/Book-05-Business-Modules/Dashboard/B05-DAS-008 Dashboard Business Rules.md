# B05-DAS-008

# Dashboard Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official dashboard rules.

---

# Rule 1

Dashboard visibility is role-based.

---

# Rule 2

Users only see authorized information.

---

# Rule 3

Dashboard data is read-only.

Operational changes occur in source modules.

---

# Rule 4

Dashboards summarize information.

They do not replace operational workflows.

---

# Rule 5

Each role receives a default dashboard.

---

# Rule 6

Users may customize dashboard layouts.

---

# Rule 7

Users may hide widgets.

---

# Rule 8

Users may reorder widgets.

---

# Rule 9

Users may restore default layouts.

---

# Rule 10

Dashboard content follows Accounting Mode.

---

# Rule 11

Alert priorities support:

* Information
* Warning
* Critical

---

# Rule 12

Dashboard information must remain auditable.

---

# Rule 13

Dashboard performance must prioritize speed.

---

# Summary

Dashboard Business Rules ensure dashboards remain useful, secure, and role-focused.
