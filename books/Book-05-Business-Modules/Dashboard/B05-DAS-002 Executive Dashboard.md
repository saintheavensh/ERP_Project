# B05-DAS-002

# Executive Dashboard

## Purpose

Provides owners and managers with high-level business visibility.

---

# Business Philosophy

Executives should understand business performance within seconds.

---

# KPI Components

---

## Revenue

Displays:

* Total Revenue
* Service Revenue
* Retail Revenue

---

## Profit Summary

Displays:

* Estimated Profit
* Gross Margin

---

## Cash Position

Displays:

* Cash Balance
* Bank Balance

---

## Service Summary

Displays:

* Open Service Orders
* Waiting Parts
* Completed Repairs

---

## Inventory Summary

Displays:

* Inventory Value
* Low Stock Products

---

## Purchasing Summary

Displays:

* Open Purchase Orders
* Outstanding Purchases

---

## Warranty Summary

Displays:

* Active Warranties
* Warranty Claims

---

# Visibility

Owner
Manager

---

# Summary

Executive Dashboard provides a high-level business overview.
