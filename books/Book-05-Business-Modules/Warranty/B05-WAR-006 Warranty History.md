# B05-WAR-006

# Warranty History Management

## Purpose

Defines warranty history tracking and warranty relationship visibility.

---

# Business Philosophy

Warranty history should provide a complete view of customer repair outcomes.

---

# History Structure

Example:

```text id="wh001"

```

---

Linked Warranty Events:

```text id="wh002"

```

---

```text id="wh003"

```

---

# Stored Information

Supports:

* Original Service Order
* Warranty Claims
* Warranty Service Orders
* Warranty Outcomes

---

# Customer Visibility

Displays:

* Active Warranty
* Previous Claims
* Warranty Expiration

---

# Technician Visibility

Displays:

* Previous Repairs
* Previous Warranty Work

---

# Reporting

Provides:

* Repeat Failure Analysis
* Warranty Frequency
* Warranty Cost Trends

---

# Summary

Warranty History Management provides complete visibility of warranty activity.
