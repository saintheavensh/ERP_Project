# B05-WAR-008

# Warranty Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official warranty management rules.

---

# Rule 1

Warranty is optional.

---

# Rule 2

Warranty is assigned per repair item.

---

# Rule 3

Warranty Templates are configurable.

---

# Rule 4

Warranty activates only after:

```text id="wbr001"
Repair Completed
↓
QC Passed
↓
Warranty Activated
```

---

# Rule 5

Warranty claims require validation.

---

# Rule 6

Warranty claims must reference an original Service Order.

---

# Rule 7

Approved warranty claims generate Warranty Service Orders.

---

# Rule 8

Warranty Service Orders remain linked to original repairs.

---

# Rule 9

Warranty repairs follow standard repair workflows.

---

# Rule 10

Warranty inventory usage remains auditable.

---

# Rule 11

Warranty costs remain traceable.

---

# Rule 12

Warranty renewal policies are configurable.

Supported:

* Follow Original Expiry
* Reset Warranty
* Custom Policy

---

# Rule 13

Warranty history must remain permanently traceable.

---

# Rule 14

Warranty metrics contribute to technician performance reporting.

---

# Rule 15

All warranty activities require audit logging.

---

# Summary

Warranty Business Rules ensure consistency, traceability, and operational control.
