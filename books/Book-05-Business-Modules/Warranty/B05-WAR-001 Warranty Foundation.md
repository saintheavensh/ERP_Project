# B05-WAR-001

# Warranty Foundation

## Purpose

Defines warranty architecture, warranty relationships, and warranty lifecycle management.

---

# Business Philosophy

Warranty is a service commitment.

Warranty should protect customers while maintaining operational control.

---

# Official Decision

Warranty is optional.

---

# Warranty Model

Supported:

```text id="wf001"
Per Repair Item
```

---

Not:

```text id="wf002"
Per Service Order Only
```

---

# Examples

### LCD Replacement

7 Days

---

### Battery Replacement

30 Days

---

### Software Service

No Warranty

---

### Cleaning Service

No Warranty

---

# Module Relationships

Warranty integrates with:

* Service
* Technician
* Customer
* Inventory
* Finance

---

# Warranty Lifecycle

```text id="wf003"
Repair
↓
QC Passed
↓
Warranty Activated
↓
Warranty Active
↓
Warranty Expired
```

---

# Core Principle

Warranty begins only after successful completion and QC approval.

---

# Summary

Warranty Foundation defines warranty architecture and lifecycle management.
