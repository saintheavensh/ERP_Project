# B05-WAR-007

# Warranty Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines warranty monitoring, claim visibility, and warranty analytics.

---

# Business Philosophy

Warranty should be measurable.

Management should understand warranty performance and warranty costs.

---

# Dashboard Components

---

## Active Warranties

Displays:

* Active Warranty Count
* Expiring Warranties
* Expired Warranties

---

## Warranty Claims

Displays:

* New Claims
* Open Claims
* Closed Claims

---

## Warranty Service Orders

Displays:

* Active Warranty Repairs
* Completed Warranty Repairs

---

## Warranty Cost Analysis

Displays:

* Parts Cost
* Labor Cost
* Total Warranty Cost

---

## Technician Analysis

Displays:

* Warranty Returns
* Repeat Failures
* Warranty Workload

---

## Product Analysis

Displays:

* Most Returned Parts
* Most Returned Repairs

---

## Customer Analysis

Displays:

* Customers With Multiple Claims
* Warranty Usage Trends

---

# Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Manager
* Service Supervisor

---

# Summary

Warranty Dashboard provides operational and financial visibility into warranty activities.
