# B05-SUP-005

# Supplier Pricing

## Purpose

Defines supplier pricing management and purchasing cost history.

---

# Business Philosophy

Different suppliers may offer different prices for the same product.

Historical pricing should be preserved.

---

# Pricing Model

Supported:

```text id="price001"
Product
↓
Supplier A
↓
Price A
```

---

```text id="price002"
Product
↓
Supplier B
↓
Price B
```

---

```text id="price003"
Product
↓
Supplier C
↓
Price C
```

---

# Price History

Supports:

* Effective Date
* Previous Price
* Current Price

---

# Supplier Cost Tracking

Tracks:

* Last Purchase Cost
* Average Cost
* Highest Cost
* Lowest Cost

---

# Purchasing Recommendations

System may recommend:

### Lowest Price

---

### Preferred Supplier

---

### Fastest Supplier

---

### Best Performance Supplier

---

# Inventory Integration

Price records do not modify historical inventory valuation.

Inventory valuation remains batch-based.

---

# Audit Trail

Track:

* Old Price
* New Price
* User
* Date

---

# Summary

Supplier Pricing provides cost visibility while preserving historical purchasing information.
