# B05-SUP-007

# Supplier Credit Terms

## Purpose

Defines supplier payment terms, due dates, credit policies, and accounts payable integration.

---

# Business Philosophy

Not all purchases are paid immediately.

Supplier credit terms provide flexibility and improve cash flow management.

---

# Supported Payment Terms

### Cash

Payment required immediately.

---

### 7 Days

Payment due within 7 days.

---

### 14 Days

Payment due within 14 days.

---

### 30 Days

Payment due within 30 days.

---

### 45 Days

Payment due within 45 days.

---

### Custom Terms

Supported.
