# B05-SUP-008

# Supplier Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines supplier operational dashboards, supplier monitoring, and purchasing visibility.

---

# Business Philosophy

Decision makers should immediately understand supplier conditions without reviewing multiple reports.

---

# Dashboard Components

---

## Supplier Overview

Displays:

* Total Suppliers
* Active Suppliers
* Inactive Suppliers
* Blacklisted Suppliers

---

## Purchasing Summary

Displays:

* Total Purchases
* Purchase Volume
* Purchase Frequency

---

## Outstanding Payables

Displays:

* Current Liabilities
* Overdue Liabilities
* Upcoming Due Payments

---

## Supplier Rankings

Displays:

* Best Suppliers
* Fastest Suppliers
* Most Used Suppliers

---

## Price Monitoring

Displays:

* Recent Price Changes
* Significant Cost Changes

---

## Product Availability

Displays:

* Frequently Out-of-Stock Suppliers
* High Availability Suppliers

---

# Dashboard Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Purchasing Team
* Finance Team

---

# Summary

Supplier Dashboard provides operational and financial supplier visibility.
