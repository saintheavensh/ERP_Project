# B05-SUP-001

# Supplier Foundation

## Purpose

Defines supplier architecture, relationships, responsibilities, and integration with purchasing and inventory operations.

---

# Business Philosophy

Suppliers are strategic business partners.

The ERP must support supplier management beyond simple contact information.

---

# Core Principle

Every purchased item should be traceable to its supplier.

---

# Supported Supplier Types

### Spare Part Supplier

Provides repair components.

---

### Device Supplier

Provides phones, tablets, laptops, and devices.

---

### Accessory Supplier

Provides accessories and consumables.

---

### Marketplace Supplier

Provides products through online marketplaces.

---

### Local Store Supplier

Provides emergency purchases from nearby stores.

---

# Module Relationships

Supplier integrates with:

* Purchasing
* Inventory
* Finance
* Service
* Reporting

---

# Supplier Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner

# Audit Trail

Track:

# Summary

Supplier Foundation establishes supplier management and supplier traceability throughout the ERP.
