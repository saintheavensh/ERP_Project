# B05-SUP-009

# Supplier Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official supplier management rules.

---

# Rule 1

Every supplier must have a unique supplier code.

---

# Rule 2

Suppliers may belong to multiple categories.

---

# Rule 3

One supplier may supply multiple products.

---

# Rule 4

One product may have multiple suppliers.

---

# Rule 5

Supplier pricing history must be preserved.

---

# Rule 6

Historical purchases must remain linked to the original supplier.

---

# Rule 7

Preferred suppliers may be defined per product.

---

# Rule 8

Supplier recommendations may consider:

* Price
* Availability
* Performance
* Delivery Speed

---

# Rule 9

Supplier payment terms may vary.

---

# Rule 10

Credit purchases create Accounts Payable obligations.

---

# Rule 11

Supplier status controls purchasing eligibility.

---

# Rule 12

Inactive suppliers cannot be used for new purchases.

---

# Rule 13

Blacklisted suppliers cannot participate in purchasing activities.

---

# Rule 14

Supplier performance data must remain auditable.

---

# Rule 15

All supplier modifications require audit tracking.

---

# Summary

Supplier Business Rules ensure consistency, accountability, and purchasing integrity.
