# B05-SUP-003

# Supplier Categories

## Purpose

Defines supplier classification and specialization.

---

# Business Philosophy

Not all suppliers provide the same products.

Categorization improves purchasing decisions.

---

# Supported Categories

### Spare Parts

Examples:

* LCD
* Battery
* IC
* Flex Cable

---

### Devices

Examples:

* Phones
* Tablets
* Laptops

---

### Accessories

Examples:

* Chargers
* Cases
* Earphones

---

### Tools

Examples:

* Soldering Equipment
* Repair Tools

---

### Services

Examples:

* Logistics
* Repair Outsourcing

---

# Multi Category Support

One supplier may belong to multiple categories.

---

# Purchasing Integration

Categories support:

* Supplier Filtering
* Purchase Recommendations
* Supplier Search

---

# Reporting

Provides:

* Supplier Category Analysis
* Category Spend Reports

---

# Summary

Supplier Categories improve supplier organization and purchasing efficiency.
