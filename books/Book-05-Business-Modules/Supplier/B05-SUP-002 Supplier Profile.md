# B05-SUP-002

# Supplier Profile Management

## Purpose

Defines supplier information, contact records, and operational details.

---

# Supplier Information

Supports:

* Supplier Code
* Supplier Name
* Contact Person
* Phone Number
* Email
* Address

---

# Tax Information

Supports:

* Tax Number
* Tax Name
* Tax Address

Optional.

---

# Banking Information

Supports:

* Bank Name
* Account Number
* Account Holder

---

# Supplier Status

### Active

Can be used for purchasing.

---

### Inactive

Cannot be used for new purchases.

---

### Blacklisted

Blocked from purchasing.

---

# Notes

Supports:

* Internal Notes
* Supplier Comments

---

# Attachments

Supports:

* Contracts
* Agreements
* Tax Documents
* Supplier Catalogs

---

# Audit Trail

Track:

* Profile Updates
* Status Changes
* Document Changes

---

# Summary

Supplier Profile Management stores operational and financial supplier information.
