# B05-INV-011

# Stock Opname

---

# Purpose

Defines physical inventory counting and reconciliation processes.

---

# Business Philosophy

Physical inventory is the ultimate source of truth.

System inventory must be periodically verified.

---

# Core Principle

Official rule:

```text id="opname001"
Physical Stock Wins
```

When discrepancies occur:

Investigation is required.

---

# Objectives

Stock Opname exists to:

* Verify inventory accuracy
* Detect losses
* Detect errors
* Improve inventory reliability

---

# Stock Opname Workflow

```text id="opname002"
Freeze Counting Scope
↓
Count Physical Inventory
↓
Compare To System
↓
Identify Variance
↓
Review
↓
Adjustment
```

---

# Example

System:

```text id="opname003"
10 pcs
```

Physical:

```text id="opname004"
8 pcs
```

Variance:

```text id="opname005"
-2 pcs
```

---

# Variance Classification

---

## Positive Variance

Physical > System

Example:

```text id="opname006"
System = 8

Physical = 10
```

---

## Negative Variance

Physical < System

Example:

```text id="opname007"
System = 10

Physical = 8
```

---

# Counting Methods

---

## Full Count

Entire inventory counted.

---

## Cycle Count

Partial inventory counted periodically.

Future recommended.

---

# Investigation Process

System should support:

* Variance Notes
* Investigation Notes
* Responsible Personnel
* Corrective Actions

---

# Adjustment Integration

Stock Opname does not directly modify inventory.

Required flow:

```text id="opname008"
Variance
↓
Review
↓
Stock Adjustment
```

---

# Approval Rules

Large variances should require:

```text id="opname009"
Management Approval
```

---

# Reporting Requirements

Support:

* Variance Reports
* Accuracy Rate
* Inventory Reliability
* Variance By Product
* Variance By Category
* Variance By User

---

# KPI Opportunities

Future KPIs:

```text id="opname010"
Inventory Accuracy %
```

```text id="opname011"
Variance Frequency
```

---

# Summary

* Physical stock is the final reference.
* Variances require investigation.
* Stock Opname does not directly edit stock.
* Adjustments handle corrections.
* Inventory accuracy reporting supported.
