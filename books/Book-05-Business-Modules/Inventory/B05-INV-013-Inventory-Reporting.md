# B05-INV-013

# Inventory Reporting

---

# Purpose

Defines inventory reporting requirements and analytical capabilities.

---

# Business Philosophy

Inventory data must support business decisions.

Inventory reporting is not limited to stock quantities.

Reports must support:

* Operations
* Purchasing
* Service
* Finance
* Management

---

# Core Principle

Official rule:

```text
Every Inventory Movement Must Be Reportable
```

---

# Operational Reports

---

## Current Stock

Displays:

* Product
* Available Quantity
* Reserved Quantity
* Total Quantity

---

## Batch Inventory

Displays:

* Batch
* Supplier
* Cost
* Remaining Quantity

---

## Inventory Ledger

Displays:

* Stock In
* Stock Out
* Adjustments
* Returns

---

# Purchasing Reports

---

## Supplier Performance

Metrics:

* Purchase Volume
* Defect Rate
* Return Rate

---

## Purchase History

Displays:

* Supplier
* Product
* Cost History

---

# Service Reports

---

## Sparepart Usage

Displays:

* Product Usage
* Technician Usage
* Service Usage

---

## Consumption Trends

Displays:

* Daily Usage
* Weekly Usage
* Monthly Usage

---

# Warranty Reports

---

## Warranty Consumption

Displays:

* Spareparts Used
* Warranty Cost

---

## Warranty Rate

Displays:

* Product Warranty Frequency

---

# Financial Reports

---

## Inventory Valuation

Uses:

```text
FIFO Cost
```

---

## Inventory Cost Analysis

Displays:

* Cost Trends
* Consumption Cost

---

## Inventory Loss Analysis

Displays:

* Scrap
* Damage
* Theft
* Adjustments

---

# Inventory Health Reports

---

## Fast Moving Items

Frequently consumed items.

---

## Slow Moving Items

Infrequently consumed items.

---

## Dead Stock

Items not consumed within a configurable period.

---

# Stock Accuracy Reports

---

## Stock Opname Accuracy

Displays:

* Variance Rate
* Inventory Accuracy %

---

# Dashboard Metrics

Support:

* Total Inventory Value
* Low Stock Count
* Critical Stock Count
* Out Of Stock Count
* Top Consumed Items
* Top Warranty Items

---

# Summary

* Inventory reporting supports all business areas.
* FIFO valuation mandatory.
* Inventory health analysis supported.
* Warranty and supplier analysis supported.
* Dashboard integration supported.
