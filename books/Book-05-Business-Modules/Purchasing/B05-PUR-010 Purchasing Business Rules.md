# B05-PUR-010

# Purchasing Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official purchasing rules governing procurement activities, approvals, receiving, supplier interactions, inventory updates, and financial integration.

---

# Rule 1

Every purchase must be traceable.

---

# Rule 2

Purchase Requests represent demand.

Purchase Orders represent commitments.

---

# Rule 3

One Purchase Request may support multiple Service Orders.

---

# Rule 4

One Purchase Order may contain multiple products.

---

# Rule 5

Partial Receiving is supported.

---

# Rule 6

Purchase Orders remain open until:

* Fully Received
* Closed Manually
* Cancelled

---

# Rule 7

Outstanding quantities become Backorders.

---

# Rule 8

Inventory updates only from received quantities.

---

# Rule 9

Accounts Payable is generated from received quantities.

---

# Rule 10

Supplier payment terms are supported.

---

# Rule 11

Purchase approvals are configurable.

Supported modes:

* No Approval
* Single Approval
* Multi-Level Approval

---

# Rule 12

Approval configuration is controlled through Settings.

---

# Rule 13

System recommendations assist purchasing decisions.

Final purchasing decisions remain human-controlled.

---

# Rule 14

Purchase Returns must remain auditable.

---

# Rule 15

Supplier performance metrics may influence purchasing recommendations.

---

# Rule 16

Waiting Parts purchases may automatically reserve inventory for Service Orders.

---

# Rule 17

External technician purchases must remain linked to Service Orders.

---

# Rule 18

All purchasing activities require audit logging.

---

# Summary

Purchasing Business Rules ensure procurement consistency, traceability, inventory accuracy, and financial integrity.
