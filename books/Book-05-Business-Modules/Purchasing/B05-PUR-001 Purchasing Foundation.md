# Purchasing Foundation

## Purpose

Defines purchasing architecture, workflows, responsibilities, and module integrations.

---

# Business Philosophy

Purchasing ensures product availability while maintaining inventory accuracy and financial control.

---

# Core Principle

Every purchased item must be traceable from request to receiving.
