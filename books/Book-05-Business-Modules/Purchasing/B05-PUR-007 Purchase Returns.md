# B05-PUR-007

# Purchase Return Management

## Purpose

Defines supplier return processes, inventory adjustments, financial corrections, and supplier return tracking.

---

# Business Philosophy

Returned products must be traceable and auditable.

Inventory and financial records must remain accurate.

---

# Return Reasons

Supports:

### Defective Product

Product is damaged or non-functional.

---

### Wrong Product

Incorrect product delivered.

---

### Wrong Quantity

Receiving discrepancy.

---

### Wrong Quality

Quality does not match expectations.

---

### Supplier Recall

Supplier requests product return.

---

### Other

Custom return reasons.

---

# Return Workflow

```text id="prt001"
Received Product
↓
Return Request
↓
Approval
↓
Supplier Return
↓
Inventory Adjustment
↓
Financial Adjustment
```

---

# Inventory Impact

Returned inventory is removed from available stock.

---

# Financial Impact

Supports:

### Credit Note

Supplier reduces payable balance.

---

### Replacement

Supplier sends replacement goods.

---

### Refund

Supplier refunds payment.
