# B05-PUR-009

# Purchasing Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines purchasing visibility, procurement monitoring, and purchasing KPIs.

---

# Business Philosophy

Management should quickly understand purchasing conditions and supplier performance.

---

# Dashboard Components

---

## Purchase Requests

Displays:

* Draft PR
* Pending Approval
* Approved PR

---

## Purchase Orders

Displays:

* Open PO
* Partial PO
* Closed PO

---

## Outstanding Orders

Displays:

* Outstanding Qty
* Backordered Qty
* Incoming Stock

---

## Supplier Performance

Displays:

* Fill Rate
* Delivery Reliability
* Return Rate

---

## Financial Summary

Displays:

* Outstanding Payables
* Upcoming Payments

---

## Waiting Parts Impact

Displays:

* Service Orders Waiting Parts
* Parts Needed
* Purchase Status

---

## Recommendations

Displays:

* Suggested Purchases
* Reorder Alerts

---

# Dashboard Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Purchasing Team
* Inventory Team

---

# Summary

Purchasing Dashboard provides operational and financial visibility across procurement activities.
