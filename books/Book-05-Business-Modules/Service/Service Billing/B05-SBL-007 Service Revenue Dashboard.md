# B05-SBL-007

# Service Revenue Dashboard

## Purpose

Defines service revenue visibility, billing analytics, and financial monitoring.

---

# Business Philosophy

Service revenue should remain visible and separate from retail sales revenue.

---

# Dashboard Components

---

## Revenue Summary

Displays:

* Daily Revenue
* Weekly Revenue
* Monthly Revenue

---

## Invoice Summary

Displays:

* Issued Invoices
* Paid Invoices
* Outstanding Invoices

---

## Deposit Summary

Displays:

* Deposits Received
* Deposits Applied
* Deposits Refunded

---

## Payment Method Analysis

Displays:

* Cash
* Transfer
* QRIS
* Card
* Mixed Payment

---

## Service Category Analysis

Displays:

* Revenue By Service Type
* Revenue By Technician

---

## Refund Analysis

Displays:

* Refund Count
* Refund Value

---

## Outstanding Collections

Displays:

* Unpaid Invoices
* Partial Payments

---

# Visibility

Visible to:

* Owner
* Finance
* Manager

---

# Summary

Service Revenue Dashboard provides visibility into service income and billing performance.
