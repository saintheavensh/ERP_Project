# B05-SBL-008

# Service Billing Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official service billing rules.

---

# Rule 1

Every billable service must be linked to a Service Order.

---

# Rule 2

Service revenue is tracked separately from retail sales revenue.

---

# Rule 3

Customer approval is required before repair work begins.

---

# Rule 4

Cost increases require customer re-approval.

---

# Rule 5

Estimate revisions create new versions.

---

# Rule 6

Previous quotation versions must remain preserved.

---

# Rule 7

Customer approvals require supporting evidence.

---

# Rule 8

Deposits are linked to Service Orders.

---

# Rule 9

Deposits reduce final invoice balances.

---

# Rule 10

Invoices support detailed and summary presentation modes.

---

# Rule 11

Mixed payment methods are supported.

---

# Rule 12

Refund policies are configurable.

---

# Rule 13

All refunds require audit tracking.

---

# Rule 14

Service billing activities require audit logging.

---

# Rule 15

All financial impacts must be recorded.

---

# Summary

Service Billing Business Rules ensure transparency, customer protection, and financial integrity.
