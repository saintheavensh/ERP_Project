# B05-SVC-CAT-009

# Service Category Business Rules

---

# Purpose

Defines the official business rules governing Service Categories.

---

# Rule 1

Every Service Order Must Have At Least One Category

Official decision:

```text id="sbr001"
Category Assignment Mandatory
```

---

# Rule 2

Multiple Categories Supported

Official decision:

A service order may contain:

```text id="sbr002"
One
or
Many
```

categories.

---

# Rule 3

Categories Drive Time Estimation

Official decision:

Default repair durations originate from categories.

---

# Rule 4

Categories Drive Warranty Defaults

Official decision:

Warranty periods originate from categories.

---

# Rule 5

Categories Drive Pricing Guidance

Official decision:

Pricing recommendations originate from categories.

---

# Rule 6

Categories Support Technician Assignment

Official decision:

Categories define skill requirements.

---

# Rule 7

Category Changes Must Not Affect Historical Records

Official decision:

Historical service orders retain original values.

---

# Rule 8

Every Category Requires Estimated Duration

Official decision:

Estimated duration is mandatory.

---

# Rule 9

Every Category Requires Warranty Configuration

Official decision:

Warranty settings must be defined.

Zero-day warranty allowed.

---

# Rule 10

Every Category Requires Pricing Configuration

Official decision:

Pricing guidance must exist.

---

# Rule 11

Waiting Parts Does Not Affect Technician KPI

Official decision:

Inventory shortages are excluded from punctuality calculations.

---

# Rule 12

Customer Approval Delays Do Not Affect Technician KPI

Official decision:

Awaiting customer decisions is excluded.

---

# Rule 13

Additional Damage Discovery Does Not Affect Technician KPI

Official decision:

Revised diagnoses are excluded.

---

# Rule 14

Categories Must Remain Reportable

Official decision:

Inactive categories remain visible historically.

---

# Rule 15

Categories Cannot Be Deleted

Official decision:

Categories may only become:

```text id="sbr003"
Inactive
```

or

```text id="sbr004"
Archived
```

---

# Rule 16

Category Codes Must Be Unique

Official decision:

Duplicate category codes prohibited.

---

# Rule 17

Owner Override Supported

Official decision:

Management may override category recommendations.

Audit trail required.

---

# Rule 18

Dashboard Widgets Depend On Category Data

Official decision:

Category metrics are primary dashboard sources.

---

# Rule 19

Category Reporting Is Mandatory

Official decision:

All category activity must remain reportable.

---

# Rule 20

Service Categories Are Shared Business Assets

Official decision:

Categories serve:

* Service
* Technician
* Inventory
* Warranty
* Reporting
* Dashboard

---

# Summary

Service Categories govern:

* Pricing
* Estimation
* Warranty
* Technician Assignment
* Reporting
* Dashboard Analytics

They are foundational business entities within Universal Service ERP.
