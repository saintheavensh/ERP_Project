# B05-SVC-CAT-006

# Technician Relationship

---

# Purpose

Defines the relationship between Service Categories and Technician Management.

---

# Business Philosophy

Not every technician should perform every repair.

Technician assignments should reflect skills and capabilities.

---

# Core Principle

Service Categories provide workload, skill, and KPI context for technicians.

---

# Skill Mapping

Categories may be linked to technician skill levels.

```text id="tr012"

```


```text id="tr014"

```


# Summary

* Service Categories define technician requirements.
* Categories drive scheduling and KPI calculations.
* Delays outside technician control should not reduce KPI performance.
* Categories support long-term skill development.
