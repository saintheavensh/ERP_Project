# B05-SVC-CAT-007

# Category Reporting

---

# Purpose

Defines reporting requirements related to Service Categories.

---

# Business Philosophy

Service Categories are not only operational classifications.

They are business intelligence assets.

Management should be able to understand:

* Which services are most requested
* Which services generate the most profit
* Which services create the most warranty claims
* Which services consume the most technician time

---

# Core Principle

Official rule:

```text id="cr001"
Every Service Category Must Be Measurable
```

---

# Operational Reports

---

## Service Volume By Category

Displays:

* Category
* Service Count
* Percentage of Total Repairs

Example:

```text id="cr002"
LCD Replacement
250 Jobs
```

```text id="cr003"
Battery Replacement
180 Jobs
```

---

## Service Trend By Category

Displays:

* Daily Trend
* Weekly Trend
* Monthly Trend

---

# Financial Reports

---

## Revenue By Category

Displays:

* Revenue
* Service Count
* Average Revenue

---

## Profit By Category

Displays:

* Revenue
* Sparepart Cost
* Labor Cost
* Net Profit

---

## Average Selling Price

Displays:

```text id="cr004"
Total Revenue
÷
Total Jobs
```

---

# Technician Reports

---

## Category Performance By Technician

Displays:

* Category
* Job Count

---

## Completion Time Analysis

Displays:

* Estimated Duration
* Actual Duration

---

## On-Time Completion Rate

Displays:

* On-Time Jobs
* Delayed Jobs

# Warranty Reports

---
