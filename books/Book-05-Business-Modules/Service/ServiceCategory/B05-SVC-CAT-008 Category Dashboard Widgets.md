# B05-SVC-CAT-008

# Category Dashboard Widgets

---

# Purpose

Defines dashboard widgets powered by Service Category data.

---

# Business Philosophy

Important business metrics should be visible immediately.

Managers should not need to open reports for common insights.

---

# Core Principle

Widgets summarize information.

Reports explain information.
