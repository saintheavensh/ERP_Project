# B05-FIN-012

# Balance Sheet

---

# Purpose

Defines asset, liability, and equity reporting.

---

# Business Philosophy

Owners should understand the financial position of the business at any point in time.

---

# Core Principle

Official Rule:

```text
Assets
=
Liabilities
+
Equity
```

---

# Accounting Mode OFF

Display:

## Financial Position Summary

Supported:

* Available Cash
* Inventory Value
* Accounts Receivable
* Accounts Payable
* Estimated Net Worth

---

Example:

```text
Available Cash
50,000,000

Inventory
100,000,000

Receivables
15,000,000

Payables
10,000,000

Estimated Net Worth
155,000,000
```

---

Detailed balance sheet hidden.

---

# Accounting Mode ON

Full Balance Sheet available.

---

# Assets

Examples:

* Cash
* Bank
* Inventory
* Accounts Receivable

---

# Liabilities

Examples:

* Supplier Payables
* Customer Deposits
* Taxes Payable

---

# Equity

Examples:

* Owner Capital
* Retained Earnings

---

# Reporting Period

Balance Sheet generated as of a specific date.

---

# Multi Branch Support

Supports:

* Branch Position
* Consolidated Position

---

# Financial Health Indicators

Provides:

* Working Capital
* Current Ratio
* Debt Position

---

# Reporting Integration

Uses:

* General Ledger
* Chart Of Accounts

---

# Audit Requirements

Every figure must trace back to source journals.

---

# Summary

Balance Sheet provides a snapshot of the organization's financial position.
