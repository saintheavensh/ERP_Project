# B05-FIN-011

# Profit & Loss Statement

---

# Purpose

Defines profitability reporting and income statement generation.

---

# Business Philosophy

Business owners need to understand whether operations generate profit.

---

# Core Principle

Profit is determined by:

```text
Revenue
-
Expenses
=
Profit
```

---

# Accounting Mode OFF

Display:

## Profit Summary

Supported:

* Revenue
* Expenses
* Estimated Profit

---

Example:

```text
Revenue
120,000,000

Expenses
70,000,000

Estimated Profit
50,000,000
```

---

Detailed account breakdown is hidden.

---

# Accounting Mode ON

Full Profit & Loss Statement available.

---

# Revenue Categories

Examples:

* Service Revenue
* Product Revenue
* Other Revenue

---

# Expense Categories

Examples:

* Salaries
* Rent
* Electricity
* Internet
* Marketing
* Transportation

---

# Reporting Periods

Supported:

* Daily
* Weekly
* Monthly
* Quarterly
* Yearly
* Custom

---

# Branch Support

Supports:

* Single Branch
* Multi Branch
* Consolidated View

---

# Comparison Support

Supports:

* Current vs Previous Period
* Branch Comparison

---

# KPI Integration

Provides:

* Gross Profit
* Net Profit
* Profit Margin

---

# Dashboard Integration

Provides:

* Profit Trend
* Revenue Trend
* Expense Trend

---

# Audit Requirements

All figures must be traceable to ledger transactions.

---

# Summary

Profit & Loss Statement measures business profitability and financial performance.
