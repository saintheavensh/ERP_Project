# B05-FIN-014

# Financial Reporting

---

# Purpose

Defines all financial reports generated by Universal Service ERP.

---

# Business Philosophy

Reports must be understandable by business owners while remaining useful for accountants.

---

# Reporting Levels

---

## Owner View

Accounting OFF

Displays simplified reports.

---

## Accounting View

Accounting ON

Displays complete financial reports.

---

# Available Reports

---

## Financial Summary

Supported:

* Revenue Summary
* Expense Summary
* Profit Summary
* Cash Summary

---

## Accounts Receivable Reports

Supported:

* Outstanding Receivables
* Aging Reports
* Collection Reports

---

## Accounts Payable Reports

Supported:

* Outstanding Payables
* Supplier Aging
* Payment Schedules

---

## Expense Reports

Supported:

* Expense By Category
* Expense By Branch
* Expense Trends

---

## Cash Reports

Supported:

* Cash Position
* Cash Movement
* Cash Reconciliation

---

## Accounting Reports

Accounting Mode ON only.

Supported:

* Trial Balance
* General Ledger
* Profit & Loss
* Balance Sheet
* Cash Flow Statement

---

# Export Formats

Supported:

Supports integration with:

Future Enhancement.
