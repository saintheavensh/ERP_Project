# B05-FIN-013

# Cash Flow Statement

---

# Purpose

Defines cash movement reporting and cash flow analysis.

---

# Business Philosophy

Profit does not always mean cash is available.

Business owners must understand where cash comes from and where cash goes.

---

# Core Principle

Cash Flow tracks actual cash movement.

---

# Accounting Mode OFF

Display:

## Cash Summary

Supported:

* Available Cash
* Cash Received
* Cash Spent
* Net Cash Position

---

Example:

```text
Cash Received
150,000,000

Cash Spent
100,000,000

Net Cash Position
50,000,000
```

---

Detailed cash flow statement hidden.

---

# Accounting Mode ON

Full Cash Flow Statement available.

---

# Cash Flow Categories

---

## Operating Activities

Examples:

* Service Revenue
* Product Sales
* Operating Expenses
* Supplier Payments

---

## Investing Activities

Examples:

* Equipment Purchases
* Asset Purchases

---

## Financing Activities

Examples:

* Owner Capital Injection
* Owner Withdrawals
* Loan Activities

# Reporting Periods

Supported:

* Monthly

* Yearly
* Custom

---

# Branch Support

* Branch Cash Flow
* Consolidated Cash Flow

---

# Cash Health Indicators

Provides:

* Operating Cash Flow
* Free Cash Flow
* Cash Burn Trend

---

# Dashboard Integration

Provides:

* Cash Trend
* Cash Position
* Cash Movement

---

# Audit Requirements

Every cash movement must trace back to ledger transactions.

---

# Summary

Cash Flow Statement explains how cash enters and leaves the business.
