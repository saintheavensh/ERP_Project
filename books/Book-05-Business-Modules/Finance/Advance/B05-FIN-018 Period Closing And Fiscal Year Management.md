# B05-FIN-018

# Period Closing And Fiscal Year Management

## Purpose

Defines monthly closing, yearly closing, fiscal year management, and period protection.

---

## Business Philosophy

Closed periods must remain stable and auditable.

---

## Supported Closings

### Monthly Closing

```text
January
↓
Closed
```

---

### Year-End Closing

```text
Fiscal Year
↓
Closed
```

---

## Closing Workflow

```text
Review
↓
Reconciliation
↓
Approval
↓
Close Period
↓
Lock
```

---

## Closed Period Rules

Users cannot:

* Edit Transactions
* Delete Transactions
* Modify Journals

within closed periods.

---

## Reopen Process

Allowed only for:

* Owner
* Finance Administrator

---

## Fiscal Year Support

### Calendar Year

```text
01 Jan
↓
31 Dec
```

---

### Custom Fiscal Year

Supported.

---

## Carry Forward

Supported:

* Cash
* Banks
* Inventory
* Receivables
* Payables
* Equity

---

## Retained Earnings

Supported.

Year-end profit may automatically move into retained earnings.

---

## Audit Trail

Track:

* Close Date
* Reopen Date
* User
* Reason

---

## Summary

Period Closing protects financial integrity while supporting long-term accounting operations.
