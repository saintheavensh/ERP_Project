# B05-FIN-020

# Multi Branch Consolidation

## Purpose

Defines branch ownership, branch accounting, consolidated reporting, and company-wide financial visibility.

---

## Business Philosophy

Business owners must be able to view:

* Individual Branch Performance
* Overall Company Performance

using a single ERP system.

---

## Official Architectural Decision

```text id="mb001"
Every Transaction
Belongs To A Branch
```

---

## Supported Modes

### Single Branch

Default Mode.

Suitable for:

* Small Businesses
* Single Store Operations

---

### Multi Branch

Optional.

Enabled through Settings.

---

## Branch Ownership

Required for:

* Service Orders
* Sales
* Purchases
* Expenses
* Cash Transactions
* Inventory Transactions

---

## Financial Visibility Levels

### Branch View

Displays:

* Branch Revenue
* Branch Expenses
* Branch Profit
* Branch Cash

---

### Consolidated View

Displays:

* Company Revenue
* Company Expenses
* Company Profit
* Company Cash

---

## Consolidated Reports

Supports:

### Consolidated Profit & Loss

---

### Consolidated Balance Sheet

---

### Consolidated Cash Flow

---

### Consolidated Financial Summary

---

## Cash Management

Supports:

### Branch Cash

Individual branch cash balances.

---

### Company Cash

Combined cash balances.

---

## Accounts Receivable

* Branch Receivables
* Consolidated Receivables

---

## Accounts Payable

* Branch Payables
* Consolidated Payables

## Inventory Visibility

Supports:

* Branch Inventory

## Dashboard Integration

Provides:

* Branch Comparison

* Cash Comparison

Works regardless of Accounting Mode visibility.

---

## Audit Trail

Track:

* Branch
* User
* Timestamp
* Source Transaction

---

## Summary

Multi Branch Consolidation allows businesses to scale across multiple locations while maintaining centralized financial control.
