# B05-FIN-017

# Capital And Owner Equity Management

## Purpose

Defines ownership capital, capital injections, owner withdrawals, and retained earnings.

---

## Business Philosophy

Owner money and business money must remain distinguishable.

---

## Supported Transactions

### Capital Injection

Examples:

* Cash Injection
* Bank Transfer
* Asset Contribution

---

### Owner Withdrawal (Prive)

Examples:

* Personal Cash Withdrawal
* Personal Expense Paid By Company

---

### Retained Earnings

Supported.

Year-end profits may be retained in business equity.

---

## Workflow

### Capital Injection

```text
Owner
↓
Capital Entry
↓
Approval
↓
Posting
```

---

### Owner Withdrawal

```text
Owner
↓
Withdrawal
↓
Approval
↓
Posting
```

---

## Restrictions

Owner Withdrawals:

* Are not Expenses
* Are not Salaries
* Are not Operational Costs

---

## Reports

Provides:

* Capital History
* Withdrawal History
* Equity Position
* Retained Earnings

---

## Summary

Capital & Equity Management separates ownership transactions from operational activities.
