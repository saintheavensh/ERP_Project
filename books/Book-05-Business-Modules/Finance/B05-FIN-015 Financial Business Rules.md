# B05-FIN-015

# Financial Business Rules

---

# Purpose

Defines official financial rules governing all financial operations.

---

# Rule 1

Every financial transaction must have an audit trail.

---

# Rule 2

Every financial transaction must reference a source document.

---

# Rule 3

Journal entries are immutable.

Official Decision:

```text
Posted Journals
Cannot Be Edited
Cannot Be Deleted
```

---

# Rule 4

Corrections require:

* Adjustment Journal
  or
* Reversal Journal

---

# Rule 5

Accounting Engine is always active.

Official Decision:

```text
Accounting Engine
Always Active
```

---

# Rule 6

Accounting Mode only affects visibility.

Official Decision:

```text
Accounting Mode
=
Visibility Layer
```

---

# Rule 7

Switching Accounting Mode ON/OFF must never:

* Recalculate historical data
* Alter balances
* Regenerate journals
* Affect reports

---

# Rule 8

Double-entry accounting is mandatory.

Official Rule:

```text
Debit
=
Credit
```

---

# Rule 9

Accounts with transaction history cannot be deleted.

Accounts may only be disabled.

---

# Rule 10

Purchasing and Expenses are separate processes.

Official Decision:

```text
Purchasing
≠
Expense
```

---

# Rule 11

Inventory purchases must go through Purchasing.

---

# Rule 12

Operational costs must go through Expense Management.

---

# Rule 13

Every branch maintains financial ownership of its transactions.

---

# Rule 14

Financial reports must be reproducible at any time.

---

# Rule 15

Every report figure must be traceable back to source transactions.

---

# Rule 16

Opening balances require authorized approval.

---

# Rule 17

Period closing prevents accidental modifications of finalized financial periods.

Future Phase Support.

---

# Rule 18

Owner withdrawals and capital injections must be tracked separately from revenue and expenses.

---

# Rule 19

Receivables and Payables must remain visible until fully settled.

---

# Rule 20

Financial integrity takes priority over convenience.

---

# Summary

These business rules ensure financial accuracy, auditability, and long-term reliability of Universal Service ERP.
