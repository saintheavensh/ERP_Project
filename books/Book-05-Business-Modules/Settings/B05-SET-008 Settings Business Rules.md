# B05-SET-008

# Settings Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines official configuration management rules.

---

# Rule 1

The system must remain operational using default settings.

---

# Rule 2

Core system logic cannot be disabled.

Examples:

* Audit Trail
* FIFO Logic
* Data Integrity Controls

---

# Rule 3

Business policies may be configured.

---

# Rule 4

Financial behavior follows Accounting Mode.

---

# Rule 5

Role permissions control access to settings.

---

# Rule 6

Sensitive settings require administrative permissions.

---

# Rule 7

Printer assignments support single-printer and multi-printer environments.

---

# Rule 8

Operational policies remain configurable.

---

# Rule 9

Financial policies remain configurable.

---

# Rule 10

Dashboard layouts may be customized.

---

# Rule 11

Users may restore default dashboard layouts.

---

# Rule 12

Notification behavior remains configurable.

---

# Rule 13

Configuration changes must be auditable.

---

# Rule 14

System defaults follow recommended business practices.

---

# Rule 15

Settings should simplify business operations, not increase complexity.

---

# Summary

Settings Business Rules ensure safe, flexible, and controlled system configuration.
