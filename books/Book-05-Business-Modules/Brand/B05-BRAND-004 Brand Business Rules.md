# B05-BRAND-004

# Brand Business Rules

---

# Purpose

Defines official business rules governing Brand management.

---

# Rule 1

Every Device Must Belong To A Brand

Official decision:

```text id="bbr001"
Brand Required
```

---

# Rule 2

Brands Cannot Be Deleted

Official decision:

Brands may become inactive.

Historical data remains.

---

# Rule 3

Brand History Must Be Preserved

Official decision:

Past transactions remain linked to original brands.

---

# Rule 4

Brand Data Supports Reporting

Official decision:

Brand metrics must remain reportable.

---

# Rule 5

Brand Codes Must Be Unique

Official decision:

Duplicate brand codes prohibited.

---

# Rule 6

Inactive Brands Remain Reportable

Official decision:

Historical visibility required.

---

# Rule 7

Brands Are Shared Business Resources

Official decision:

Brands serve:

* Device
* Service
* Inventory
* Sales
* Reporting
* Dashboard

---

# Summary

Brand management emphasizes consistency, traceability, and business analytics.
