# Brand Foundation

---

# Purpose

Defines Brand entities and their role within Universal Service ERP.

---

# Business Philosophy

Brands are strategic business dimensions.

Brands influence:

* Service Demand
* Inventory Planning
* Supplier Relationships
* Sales Performance
* Warranty Analysis

---

# Core Principle

Every device should belong to a brand.
