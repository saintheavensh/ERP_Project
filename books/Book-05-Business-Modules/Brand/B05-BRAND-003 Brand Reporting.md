# B05-BRAND-003

# Brand Reporting

---

# Purpose

Defines reporting requirements related to brands.

---

# Business Philosophy

Management should understand business performance by brand.

---

# Core Principle

Every transaction associated with a device should contribute to brand analytics.

---

# Service Reports

---

## Service Volume By Brand

Displays:

* Brand
* Repair Count

---

## Revenue By Brand

Displays:

Revenue generated per brand.

---

## Warranty By Brand

Displays:

Warranty frequency.

---

# Inventory Reports

---

## Inventory Value By Brand

Displays:

Inventory investment per brand.

---

## Sparepart Consumption By Brand

Displays:

Inventory usage patterns.

---

# Sales Reports

---

## Sales By Brand

Displays:

Sales volume and revenue.

---

# Management Reports

---

## Most Profitable Brand

Displays:

Highest profit contribution.

---

## Highest Warranty Brand

Displays:

Brands generating the most warranty activity.

---

## Fastest Growing Brand

Displays:

Growth trends.

---

# Dashboard Usage

Brand reporting supports:

* Executive Dashboards
* Operational Dashboards
* Service Dashboards

---

# Summary

Brand reporting enables strategic business analysis across the entire ERP.
