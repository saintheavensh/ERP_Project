# System Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the architectural principles that guide the design and implementation of the Universal Service ERP platform.

---

# Principles

## Modularity

The platform is composed of independent modules with clearly defined responsibilities.

---

## Separation of Concerns

Each layer and module is responsible for a specific concern.

---

## Loose Coupling

Modules should communicate through defined interfaces without depending on internal implementations.

---

## High Cohesion

Related functionality should remain within the same module.

---

## Reusability

Shared functionality should be implemented once and reused across the platform.

---

## Extensibility

The architecture should allow new modules to be added without modifying existing modules.

---

## Maintainability

The platform should remain easy to understand, modify, and test.

---

## Scalability

The architecture should support increasing business complexity and future growth.

---

# Summary

These principles guide every architectural decision within the Universal Service ERP platform.
